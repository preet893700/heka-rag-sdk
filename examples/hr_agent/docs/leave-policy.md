# Leave Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2025. This policy applies to all full-time employees of Acme Corp.

## Casual leave

Full-time employees receive 12 days of casual leave per calendar year, credited on 1 January. Casual leave cannot be taken for more than 3 consecutive days without manager approval. Unused casual leave does not carry over to the next year.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Up to 5 unused sick leave days carry over to the next year.

## Earned leave

Employees earn 1.5 days of earned leave for each completed month of service, which is 18 days per year. Earned leave can be carried over up to a maximum balance of 30 days. Earned leave may be encashed only at resignation or retirement; it cannot be encashed during employment.

## Maternity and paternity leave

Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Paternity leave is 10 working days, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.

## Applying for leave

Apply through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave. Emergency leave should be reported to your manager by phone or message on the first day of absence.
