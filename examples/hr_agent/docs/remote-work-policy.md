# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed 6 months of service are eligible for hybrid work. Employees in their first 6 months are expected to work from the office.

## Hybrid schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Team-wide in-office days are Tuesday and Thursday.

## Working from another city or country

Working remotely from a different city within the country for up to 15 days per year requires manager approval. Working from another country is not permitted without written approval from HR and Legal, because of tax and immigration rules.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a monitor. Eligible employees can claim a one-time home-office allowance of USD 300 for a desk or chair.
