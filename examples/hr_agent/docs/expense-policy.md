# Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense date through the finance portal. Claims older than 30 days are rejected. Attach itemized receipts for every claim above USD 25.

## Travel

Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights over 6 hours. The hotel limit is USD 180 per night in tier-1 cities and USD 120 per night elsewhere.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance during business travel is USD 60. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Approval limits

A manager can approve expenses up to USD 1,000. Expenses from USD 1,000 to USD 5,000 need director approval. Anything above USD 5,000 requires VP approval.
