# First Week Checklist

## Day 1

Collect your laptop from the IT desk (Floor 2, Room 204) between 9:30 and 11:00. Sign the confidentiality agreement with People Ops before receiving your building badge. You will meet your onboarding buddy at 14:00.

## Security training

Complete the mandatory security awareness training within 5 working days of your start date. Access to production systems stays locked until the training is complete.

## 30-60-90 plan

Meet your manager during the first week to agree on your 30-60-90 day plan. Your first performance check-in is at the end of day 90.
