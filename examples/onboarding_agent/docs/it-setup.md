# IT Setup Guide

## VPN

Install the AcmeConnect client from the software portal, then sign in with your company email. The VPN is required to reach internal tools when you are not in the office.

## Multi-factor authentication

Set up multi-factor authentication with an authenticator app on your first login. SMS codes are not supported. If you lose your phone, contact the IT helpdesk to reset your factor.

## Passwords

Reset a forgotten password at id.acme.example. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and expire every 180 days.

## Getting help

Call the IT helpdesk on extension 4357 or email helpdesk@acme.example. The helpdesk is staffed from 8:00 to 18:00 on working days.
