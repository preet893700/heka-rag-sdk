# India Country Supplement

This supplement sets out the terms that apply to employees based in India. It should be read
together with the global handbook.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees in India are entitled to 29 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and requests are made through the HR portal at least 4 working days in advance.

## Public holidays

India follows a local calendar of 8 paid public holidays. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off within 6 weeks.

## Notice period

After probation, either party must give 30 days written notice of resignation or termination in India.

## Probation

New hires in India serve a probation period of 4 months, during which the notice period is 15 days.

## Overtime

Approved overtime in India is paid at 2.0 times the base hourly rate. Overtime above 47 hours per month needs director approval.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers in India receive 32 weeks of paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks.

## Health insurance

The company health plan in India covers employees and includes an annual limit of 250,000 dollars.

## Remote work

Employees in India may work remotely up to 2 days per week after completing probation.
