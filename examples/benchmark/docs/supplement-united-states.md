# United States Country Supplement

This supplement sets out the terms that apply to employees based in United States. It should be read
together with the global handbook.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees in United States are entitled to 23 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and requests are made through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance.

## Public holidays

United States follows a local calendar of 14 paid public holidays. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off within 7 weeks.

## Notice period

After probation, either party must give 14 days written notice of resignation or termination in United States.

## Probation

New hires in United States serve a probation period of 3 months, during which the notice period is 15 days.

## Overtime

Approved overtime in United States is paid at 1.25 times the base hourly rate. Overtime above 43 hours per month needs director approval.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers in United States receive 49 weeks of paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 1 weeks.

## Health insurance

The company health plan in United States covers employees and includes an annual limit of 250,000 dollars.

## Remote work

Employees in United States may work remotely up to 1 days per week after completing probation.
