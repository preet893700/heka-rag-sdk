# Singapore Country Supplement

This supplement sets out the terms that apply to employees based in Singapore. It should be read
together with the global handbook.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees in Singapore are entitled to 23 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and requests are made through the HR portal at least 7 working days in advance.

## Public holidays

Singapore follows a local calendar of 11 paid public holidays. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off within 8 weeks.

## Notice period

After probation, either party must give 30 days written notice of resignation or termination in Singapore.

## Probation

New hires in Singapore serve a probation period of 6 months, during which the notice period is 7 days.

## Overtime

Approved overtime in Singapore is paid at 1.25 times the base hourly rate. Overtime above 56 hours per month needs director approval.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers in Singapore receive 31 weeks of paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 9 weeks.

## Health insurance

The company health plan in Singapore covers employees and includes an annual limit of 150,000 dollars.

## Remote work

Employees in Singapore may work remotely up to 2 days per week after completing probation.
