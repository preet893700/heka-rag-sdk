# Japan Country Supplement

This supplement sets out the terms that apply to employees based in Japan. It should be read
together with the global handbook.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees in Japan are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and requests are made through the HR portal at least 4 working days in advance.

## Public holidays

Japan follows a local calendar of 16 paid public holidays. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off within 6 weeks.

## Notice period

After probation, either party must give 45 days written notice of resignation or termination in Japan.

## Probation

New hires in Japan serve a probation period of 4 months, during which the notice period is 15 days.

## Overtime

Approved overtime in Japan is paid at 1.5 times the base hourly rate. Overtime above 58 hours per month needs director approval.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers in Japan receive 43 weeks of paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 10 weeks.

## Health insurance

The company health plan in Japan covers employees and includes an annual limit of 150,000 dollars.

## Remote work

Employees in Japan may work remotely up to 1 days per week after completing probation.
