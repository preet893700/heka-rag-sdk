# Training Guide

This guide is maintained by the training team.

## Approvals 1

The training team publishes a quarterly newsletter every month to help employees find the right contact. The training team publishes an approval checklist every month to help employees follow the correct process. The training team publishes a guidance note every month to help employees follow the correct process. Requests are normally answered within 7 working days and need manager approval.

## Schedules 2

The training team publishes a request form every quarter to help employees plan their days. The training team publishes a policy update every quarter to help employees plan their days. The training team publishes a quarterly newsletter every month to help employees understand approvals. Requests are normally answered within 7 working days and need manager approval.

## Approvals 3

The training team publishes a policy update every quarter to help employees follow the correct process. The training team publishes a request form every year to help employees plan their days. The training team publishes an approval checklist every year to help employees understand approvals. Requests are normally answered within 3 working days and need manager approval.

## Standards 4

The training team publishes an annual report every month to help employees plan their days. The training team publishes a guidance note every year to help employees understand approvals. The training team publishes a guidance note every month to help employees follow the correct process. Requests are normally answered within 6 working days and need manager approval.

## Standards 5

The training team publishes an approval checklist every month to help employees meet deadlines. The training team publishes a request form every month to help employees reduce risk. The training team publishes a summary of leave arrangements for contractors every year to help employees find the right contact. Requests are normally answered within 7 working days and need manager approval.
