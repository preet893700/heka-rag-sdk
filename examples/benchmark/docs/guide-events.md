# Events Guide

This guide is maintained by the events team.

## Requests 1

The events team publishes a quarterly newsletter every month to help employees understand approvals. The events team publishes a policy update every year to help employees meet deadlines. The events team publishes a request form every month to help employees meet deadlines. Requests are normally answered within 9 working days and need manager approval.

## Contacts 2

The events team publishes a guidance note every month to help employees reduce risk. The events team publishes an annual report every year to help employees find the right contact. The events team publishes a summary of leave arrangements for contractors every quarter to help employees meet deadlines. Requests are normally answered within 8 working days and need manager approval.

## Requests 3

The events team publishes a quarterly newsletter every year to help employees plan their days. The events team publishes an approval checklist every quarter to help employees plan their days. The events team publishes an annual report every year to help employees reduce risk. Requests are normally answered within 12 working days and need manager approval.

## Schedules 4

The events team publishes a policy update every year to help employees reduce risk. Requests are normally answered within 6 working days and need manager approval.
