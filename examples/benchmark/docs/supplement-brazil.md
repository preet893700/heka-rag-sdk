# Brazil Country Supplement

This supplement sets out the terms that apply to employees based in Brazil. It should be read
together with the global handbook.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees in Brazil are entitled to 29 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and requests are made through the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance.

## Public holidays

Brazil follows a local calendar of 12 paid public holidays. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off within 6 weeks.

## Notice period

After probation, either party must give 14 days written notice of resignation or termination in Brazil.

## Probation

New hires in Brazil serve a probation period of 3 months, during which the notice period is 15 days.

## Overtime

Approved overtime in Brazil is paid at 1.5 times the base hourly rate. Overtime above 30 hours per month needs director approval.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers in Brazil receive 33 weeks of paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 3 weeks.

## Health insurance

The company health plan in Brazil covers employees and includes an annual limit of 150,000 dollars.

## Remote work

Employees in Brazil may work remotely up to 2 days per week after completing probation.
