# Procurement Guide

This guide is maintained by the procurement team.

## Standards 1

The procurement team publishes an annual report every month to help employees find the right contact. The procurement team publishes a summary of leave arrangements for contractors every quarter to help employees reduce risk. The procurement team publishes a policy update every year to help employees understand approvals. Requests are normally answered within 8 working days and need manager approval.

## Schedules 2

The procurement team publishes an annual report every month to help employees find the right contact. The procurement team publishes an approval checklist every quarter to help employees reduce risk. The procurement team publishes a quarterly newsletter every month to help employees reduce risk. Requests are normally answered within 8 working days and need manager approval.

## Schedules 3

The procurement team publishes a guidance note every year to help employees understand approvals. The procurement team publishes a summary of leave arrangements for contractors every month to help employees plan their days. The procurement team publishes an approval checklist every month to help employees plan their days. Requests are normally answered within 11 working days and need manager approval.

## Contacts 4

The procurement team publishes an annual report every year to help employees plan their days. The procurement team publishes a request form every year to help employees find the right contact. The procurement team publishes a summary of leave arrangements for contractors every quarter to help employees plan their days. Requests are normally answered within 10 working days and need manager approval.

## Schedules 5

The procurement team publishes an approval checklist every month to help employees understand approvals. The procurement team publishes an annual report every year to help employees reduce risk. The procurement team publishes a policy update every year to help employees reduce risk. Requests are normally answered within 4 working days and need manager approval.
