# Germany Country Supplement

This supplement sets out the terms that apply to employees based in Germany. It should be read
together with the global handbook.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees in Germany are entitled to 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and requests are made through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance.

## Public holidays

Germany follows a local calendar of 14 paid public holidays. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off within 2 weeks.

## Notice period

After probation, either party must give 30 days written notice of resignation or termination in Germany.

## Probation

New hires in Germany serve a probation period of 3 months, during which the notice period is 15 days.

## Overtime

Approved overtime in Germany is paid at 1.25 times the base hourly rate. Overtime above 38 hours per month needs director approval.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers in Germany receive 38 weeks of paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 3 weeks.

## Health insurance

The company health plan in Germany covers employees and includes an annual limit of 250,000 dollars.

## Remote work

Employees in Germany may work remotely up to 1 days per week after completing probation.
