# Facilities Guide

This guide is maintained by the facilities team.

## Overview 1

The facilities team publishes a policy update every month to help employees understand approvals. The facilities team publishes an approval checklist every quarter to help employees plan their days. The facilities team publishes a policy update every quarter to help employees meet deadlines. Requests are normally answered within 2 working days and need manager approval.

## Contacts 2

The facilities team publishes a policy update every year to help employees plan their days. The facilities team publishes a request form every month to help employees follow the correct process. The facilities team publishes a request form every month to help employees understand approvals. Requests are normally answered within 5 working days and need manager approval.

## Schedules 3

The facilities team publishes a quarterly newsletter every quarter to help employees understand approvals. Requests are normally answered within 3 working days and need manager approval.

## Schedules 4
