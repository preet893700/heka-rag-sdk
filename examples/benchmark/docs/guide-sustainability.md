# Sustainability Guide

This guide is maintained by the sustainability team.

## Overview 1

The sustainability team publishes a policy update every month to help employees plan their days. The sustainability team publishes a quarterly newsletter every month to help employees understand approvals. The sustainability team publishes a request form every quarter to help employees meet deadlines. Requests are normally answered within 2 working days and need manager approval.

## Contacts 2

The sustainability team publishes a guidance note every quarter to help employees meet deadlines. The sustainability team publishes a request form every year to help employees meet deadlines. The sustainability team publishes an approval checklist every year to help employees follow the correct process. Requests are normally answered within 9 working days and need manager approval.

## Requests 3

The sustainability team publishes a request form every quarter to help employees meet deadlines. The sustainability team publishes an approval checklist every year to help employees meet deadlines. The sustainability team publishes a quarterly newsletter every year to help employees plan their days. Requests are normally answered within 9 working days and need manager approval.

## Requests 4

The sustainability team publishes a guidance note every month to help employees find the right contact. The sustainability team publishes a guidance note every quarter to help employees understand approvals. The sustainability team publishes a summary of leave arrangements for contractors every month to help employees find the right contact. Requests are normally answered within 3 working days and need manager approval.

## Approvals 5

The sustainability team publishes a summary of leave arrangements for contractors every quarter to help employees understand approvals. The sustainability team publishes an annual report every month to help employees reduce risk. The sustainability team publishes a summary of leave arrangements for contractors every year to help employees follow the correct process. Requests are normally answered within 4 working days and need manager approval.
