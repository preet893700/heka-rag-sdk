# Finance Operations Guide

This guide is maintained by the finance operations team.

## Overview 1

The finance operations team publishes a guidance note every quarter to help employees find the right contact. The finance operations team publishes an annual report every month to help employees meet deadlines. The finance operations team publishes an approval checklist every quarter to help employees understand approvals. Requests are normally answered within 9 working days and need manager approval.

## Overview 2

The finance operations team publishes a quarterly newsletter every quarter to help employees understand approvals. The finance operations team publishes an annual report every year to help employees find the right contact. The finance operations team publishes a quarterly newsletter every quarter to help employees plan their days. Requests are normally answered within 5 working days and need manager approval.

## Contacts 3

The finance operations team publishes a request form every month to help employees plan their days. The finance operations team publishes a summary of leave arrangements for contractors every year to help employees follow the correct process. The finance operations team publishes a quarterly newsletter every month to help employees meet deadlines. Requests are normally answered within 12 working days and need manager approval.

## Overview 4

The finance operations team publishes a quarterly newsletter every month to help employees reduce risk. The finance operations team publishes a quarterly newsletter every month to help employees find the right contact. The finance operations team publishes a guidance note every quarter to help employees understand approvals. Requests are normally answered within 4 working days and need manager approval.

## Approvals 5

The finance operations team publishes a guidance note every year to help employees find the right contact. The finance operations team publishes a guidance note every quarter to help employees reduce risk. The finance operations team publishes an approval checklist every quarter to help employees follow the correct process. Requests are normally answered within 8 working days and need manager approval.
