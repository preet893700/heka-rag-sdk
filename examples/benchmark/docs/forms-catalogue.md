# HR Forms Catalogue

Every HR request starts with a numbered form. Find the form below and send it to People Operations.

### Form HR-104: Change of personal details

Use form HR-104 to update your home address, bank account or emergency contact. Changes take effect from the next payroll run.

### Form HR-118: Leave encashment

Use form HR-118 to convert unused earned leave into pay at year end. Requests must reach payroll before 15 December.

### Form HR-207: Parental leave extension

Use form HR-207 to extend parental leave beyond the standard entitlement. Submit it four weeks before your leave is due to end.

### Form HR-215: Return to work after long leave

Use form HR-215 to arrange a phased return after an absence of more than three months. Your manager and an HR partner must both sign it.

### Form HR-233: Flexible working arrangement

Use form HR-233 to propose a permanent change to your working pattern. Decisions are made within thirty days.

### Form HR-256: Internal transfer request

Use form HR-256 to apply to move to a different team or office. Your current manager is informed only after the receiving team agrees.

### Form HR-274: Relocation assistance claim

Use form HR-274 to claim moving costs when the company relocates you. Original invoices must be attached.

### Form HR-289: Tuition reimbursement claim

Use form HR-289 to claim back the cost of approved job-related courses. Attach proof of successful completion.

### Form HR-301: Grievance submission

Use form HR-301 to raise a formal complaint about your treatment at work. People Operations acknowledges receipt within two working days.

### Form HR-311: Sabbatical request

Use form HR-311 to request an unpaid sabbatical of between one and six months. Submit it at least sixty days before the intended start date.

### Form HR-327: Resignation notice

Use form HR-327 to formally give notice that you are leaving the company. Your notice period starts on the date HR stamps the form.

### Form HR-342: Exit clearance

Use form HR-342 to confirm you have returned equipment and settled advances. Final pay is released only after clearance is complete.

### Form HR-358: Referral bonus claim

Use form HR-358 to claim a bonus for a successful employee referral. The referred hire must complete six months of service first.

### Form HR-366: Equipment return

Use form HR-366 to return a laptop or phone to the IT service desk. A receipt is issued for every returned item.

### Form HR-371: Secondment application

Use form HR-371 to apply for a temporary assignment with a partner organisation. Secondments last between three and twelve months.

### Form HR-388: Performance review appeal

Use form HR-388 to challenge the outcome of your annual performance review. Appeals must be filed within ten working days of the review.

### Form HR-402: Wellness stipend claim

Use form HR-402 to claim your monthly wellness stipend. Claims are paid together with your salary.

### Form HR-419: Dependent enrolment

Use form HR-419 to add a spouse or child to your health insurance. Enrolment is possible within thirty days of a life event.

### Form HR-433: Conflict of interest declaration

Use form HR-433 to declare a personal interest that could affect your work. Declarations are reviewed by the Compliance team.

### Form HR-447: Remote work equipment allowance

Use form HR-447 to claim the one-time allowance for a desk or chair. The allowance is limited to three hundred dollars.
