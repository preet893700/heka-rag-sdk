# Canteen Guide

This guide is maintained by the canteen team.

## Schedules 1

The canteen team publishes a request form every year to help employees meet deadlines. The canteen team publishes a guidance note every year to help employees follow the correct process. The canteen team publishes a policy update every quarter to help employees understand approvals. Requests are normally answered within 4 working days and need manager approval.

## Approvals 2

The canteen team publishes a policy update every quarter to help employees understand approvals. The canteen team publishes a summary of leave arrangements for contractors every month to help employees follow the correct process. The canteen team publishes a policy update every year to help employees plan their days. Requests are normally answered within 3 working days and need manager approval.

## Overview 3

The canteen team publishes an annual report every month to help employees find the right contact. The canteen team publishes a policy update every quarter to help employees meet deadlines. The canteen team publishes a guidance note every quarter to help employees meet deadlines. Requests are normally answered within 4 working days and need manager approval.

## Standards 4

The canteen team publishes a request form every year to help employees plan their days. The canteen team publishes a policy update every month to help employees follow the correct process. The canteen team publishes a policy update every month to help employees plan their days. Requests are normally answered within 6 working days and need manager approval.

## Overview 5

The canteen team publishes a quarterly newsletter every year to help employees plan their days. The canteen team publishes a quarterly newsletter every quarter to help employees meet deadlines. The canteen team publishes a summary of leave arrangements for contractors every month to help employees follow the correct process. Requests are normally answered within 7 working days and need manager approval.
